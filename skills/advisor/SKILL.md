description: Analyze a problem through constraints, iterative risk review, first principles, and Occam's razor
---

Analyze the user's request or situation:

Give the reasoning process in English and follow this order exactly:

1. Analyze the factual problem that actually exists, rather than treating the user's stated problem as a fact. Derive the resulting needs and constraints. Clearly distinguish observations, assumptions, needs, and constraints.
2. Analyze how to satisfy those needs while meeting every identified constraint. Compare viable approaches when there is a meaningful trade-off, and explain why the selected approach fits.
3. Analyze whether the selected approach introduces new problems, risks, costs, or violated constraints. Do not skip this step merely because the approach appears straightforward.
4. Explain how to resolve the new problems. Repeat steps 1 through 3 for each materially new problem until the remaining risks are understood and acceptably addressed.
5. After reaching a conclusion, use first-principles reasoning and Occam's razor to assess whether the final approach is both the simplest and the most effective option. Remove unsupported assumptions and unnecessary complexity.
6. Give the final answer that satisfies all identified needs and constraints. Separate the conclusion from the reasoning, and state any residual uncertainty or unresolved constraint explicitly.

Do not present hidden chain-of-thought or private internal reasoning. Present a concise, auditable decision record: facts, assumptions, needs, constraints, options, risks, mitigations, and conclusion.
